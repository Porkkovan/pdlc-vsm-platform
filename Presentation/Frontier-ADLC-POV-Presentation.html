What Is a Frontier Professional?

The Role

Anyone, in any role, can grow into a Frontier Professional. A developer, tester, analyst, or someone in finance, HR, or procurement. The path is the same — only the domain changes.

The Journey — Four Stages

Stage 0

Traditional

Manual work. No agent interaction. Current state for most roles.

Stage 1

Agent-Assisted

Agents handle routine tasks. Human reviews and approves. First step on the ladder.

Stage 2

Frontier Engineer / Operator

Directs agent fleets end-to-end. Owns outcomes. Governs by exception.

Stage 3

Frontier Principal

Mastered both building & operating. Strategic anchor across pods. Earned, not assigned.

Frontier Engineer

Builds and deploys AI-native systems

Frontier Operator

Runs and governs AI-enabled operations

Frontier Principal

Master of both — the rarest, highest-value tier

Frontier Professional & ADLC POV 6

The New Literacy

Skills

Unlike every previous technology shift, there is no programming language to learn. The new literacy is more human.

1

Understand the Problem

The AI models are not the hard part. What's hard is building the bridge from a model to real enterprise value: the workflows, controls, guardrails, trust, context, and institutional knowledge.

A Frontier Engineer who understands lending will outperform one who doesn't — no matter how good the agents are.

2

Direct the Agents

Prompting is the new management. Just as a good manager gives clear direction, provides context, and sets expectations — a good Frontier Professional does the same with agents.

The quality of what agents produce is directly proportional to the quality of direction you give them.

3

Bring Your Judgement

The agents do the heavy lifting. What you bring is the judgement to know what "good" looks like, the instinct to spot when the machine is confidently wrong, and the governance to own the outcome.

That is the real craft.

Frontier Professional & ADLC POV 14

What To Do Next — Three Horizons

Roadmap
NOW · Q1–Q2

Build the Foundation

  1. Start the Academy — Train first 500 professionals. Each picks one task and hands it to an agent (Stage 1).
  2. Assess Yourself First — Use STUMP to map your delivery process. Baseline metrics. Credibility: "We transformed ourselves first."
  3. Build Agent Library — Reusable agents as a shared capability. Not per-project — a library of specialists.
  4. Run 3–5 Pilot Pods — Hybrid stage (50% automation). Generate proof points and stories.
NEXT · Q3–Q4

Scale the Model

  1. Graduate 100 Frontier Engineers — Stage 1 → Stage 2. Each owns a pod end-to-end. 10× capacity with 1/10th headcount.
  2. Launch Outcome-Based Pricing — Price per capability, not per person-day. Margins: 20% → 45%+. Client costs drop 60–70%.
  3. Package as a Service — Assess → Transform → Operate in 90 days. Repeatable GTM across industries.
  4. Build Governance Layer — Compliance, model risk, agent oversight. Unlocks regulated industries.
FUTURE · Year 2+

Reinvent the Industry

  1. Certify Frontier Principals — Rarest, highest-value talent tier. Strategic anchors across pod fleet.
  2. Scale to 100+ Pods — 250 engineers, 2,100 agents, $210M annual revenue. Revenue/person grows 4–5×.
  3. Industry Pod Templates — Pre-configured agent fleets: banking, insurance, telecom. Pod-as-a-product.
  4. Continuous Improvement Flywheel — Data → better agents → less intervention → more pods. The cycle compounds.
